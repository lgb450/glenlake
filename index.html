How to Play

Learn

  • Pick a play with the arrows, or tap the play name to see them all.
  • Choose Our zone or Their zone, and Left or Right.
  • Tap a player to see where they go and why. Tap more players to add them.
  • Whole Team shows or hides all of your players. Other Team does the same for the other team. Clear takes everyone off the ice except the puck carrier.
  • In Their zone, open Win the puck to watch F1 pressure, F2 support, and F3 get open once F1 wins the puck.
  • In Their zone, open the Rotation play and tap Next to watch the forwards switch jobs when the puck moves to the other corner.
  • Plays in red are when they have the puck. Plays in green are when we have it.

Play

  • Pick a zone and a position, then tap the spot on the ice where your player should go.
  • After you answer, you’ll see where everyone goes.
  • Earn points for every good spot. Keep a streak going for bonus points!

Challenge

  • Everyone gets the same 10 plays, in the same order, so scores are fair.
  • Add your jersey number on the home screen to post your best score to the team leaderboard. Only your best score counts, so try again to beat it!
  • Play is for practice and does not go on the leaderboard.
